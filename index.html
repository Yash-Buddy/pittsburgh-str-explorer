Pittsburgh Short-Term Rental Data Exploration

A hand-checked, listing-level census of short-term rentals across four City of Pittsburgh neighbourhoods — Lower, Central and Upper Lawrenceville, and Bloomfield — compiled while Council Bill 2026-0009 (the short-term rental zoning bill) was pending.

0. Where exactly is every listing?

Real City of Pittsburgh neighbourhood boundaries, shaded by live listing count (darker = more listings), with every listing plotted on top at its actual coordinates. 63 of the 273 live Airbnb rows are flagged as materially uncertain about which side of a neighbourhood boundary they fall on — shown here with a dashed ring, per the source dataset's own caveat.

Coordinates are platform-displaced by roughly 150m, per the source dataset's own caveat — treat exact pin positions as approximate, not precise addresses. Delisted rows are excluded; hotel-style rooms are included and marked distinctly. Neighbourhood boundaries: City of Pittsburgh Neighborhoods layer, via WPRDC / ArcGIS Hub, fetched live in your browser.

2. How do rates differ by neighbourhood and season?

Median observed 2-night, 2-guest stay totals across three date windows: a mid-September weekday stay, a late-September weekend, and a December weekend.

Rates are stay totals, not nightly rates, and are never averaged across windows — each bar is an independent median for that specific date window, computed only from listings with a value for it.
